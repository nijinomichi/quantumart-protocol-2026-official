## Interpretation

The **Earth/Mars Axis** is a conceptual metaphor.

- **Earth** symbolizes accumulated memory, observation, and historical experience.
- **Mars** symbolizes imagined futures, intention, creativity, and unrealized possibilities.

The arrow **Future ⇄ Present** does **not** represent physical backward causation.

Instead, it illustrates how imagined futures influence present decisions, artistic creation, and human meaning-making.

The "Present" is interpreted as the resonance point where memory and future possibility intersect.

---

## Scientific Position

This framework is informed by ideas originating from

- Quantum state spaces
- Time-symmetric formulations in physics
- Predictive processing in cognitive science
- Philosophy of time

However, it **does not claim** that these scientific concepts demonstrate physical retrocausality or validate the framework as a physical theory.

The work should be understood as a **Quantum Aesthetic Framework** for contemporary art.
