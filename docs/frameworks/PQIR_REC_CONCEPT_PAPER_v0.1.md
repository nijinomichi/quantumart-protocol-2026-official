## Abstract

This paper explores the relationships among time, consciousness, matter, and creative agency through two conceptual models: **PQIR** (*Personality Quantum Information Retrocausal Transmission*) and **REC** (*Radicant Entropic Cosmology*).

It does not propose an established physical theory. Instead, it presents a cognitive and philosophical thought experiment that treats the ordinary human world as a **middle-range observational reference** between microscopic and macroscopic regimes.

From this reference point, the paper examines two contrasting directions:

- **miniaturization, acceleration, and microscopic change**, and
- **expansion, slowing, and macroscopic change**.

The central question is whether transformations across spatial and temporal scales may help us think about how past, present, and future conditions participate in one another. The paper calls this provisional relation **Scale–Time Reciprocity**.

---

## 1. Introduction and Background

For many years, I have returned to one question:

> **Is consciousness trapped within time?**

The conventional **arrow of time** describes time as moving from past to future. **Continuity of consciousness** refers to our experience of an apparently unbroken present, while **causality** is ordinarily understood as the principle that causes precede effects.

These frameworks are indispensable, but they may not exhaust the relationships among consciousness, matter, scale, and time.

This paper therefore begins from a specific observational premise:

> The ordinary scale of human perception and action is treated as a middle-range reference rather than as an absolute center of reality.

From that middle range, the paper considers two directions:

1. toward processes that are smaller and faster than ordinary human perception;
2. toward processes that are larger and slower than ordinary human perception.

**PQIR** is a conceptual model proposed in this paper. It is not an experimentally established theory. It explores whether information associated with consciousness or personality can be modeled through the language of quantum information and temporal reversal.

**REC** is a conceptual framework for describing relations among entropy, multiple possible world-states, material transformation, order, and disorder.

## 3. The Quantum-Facing Direction

### 3.1 Miniaturization and Acceleration

In the quantum-facing direction, phenomena are modeled as becoming smaller in spatial scale and faster in local dynamics.

From the human middle range, quantum phenomena are not ordinarily perceived directly. They are described through probabilities, superposition, nonlocal correlations, and rapid state transitions.

The conceptual path is:

```text
Human present
    ↓
Miniaturization
    ↓
Acceleration
    ↓
Future-oriented spacetime
    ↓
Transformation and return
    ↓
Slowing
    ↓
Appearance within the human-observable present
```

“Future-oriented spacetime” is not used here as the name of an established physical domain. It refers to a hypothetical temporal structure in which processes occurring too rapidly or too microscopically for direct human perception become observable only after transformation and slowing.

The return is represented as a boomerang-like temporal path: a process leaves the ordinary human range, undergoes transformation, and later appears within that range as an observable present condition.

## 4. The Classical-Facing Direction

### 4.1 Expansion and Slowing

In the classical-facing direction, phenomena are modeled as becoming larger in scale and slower in observable dynamics.

Stars, planets, atmospheres, geological systems, forests, ecosystems, and civilizations unfold across durations that exceed the scale of individual human experience.

The conceptual path is:

```text
Human present
    ↓
Expansion
    ↓
Slowing
    ↓
Past-oriented spacetime
    ↓
Decay, dispersion, and decomposition
    ↓
Miniaturization
    ↓
Appearance as a future environmental condition
```

Macroscopic structures undergo decay, combustion, erosion, decomposition, and dispersion. A large structure may be transformed into gases, particles, heat, ash, molecules, and elements.

Those smaller components may later enter atmospheric, geological, hydrological, and biological cycles. In this sense, a past macroscopic structure can become part of the material conditions of the future.

## 5. Scale–Time Reciprocity

This paper does not claim that miniaturization, expansion, acceleration, slowing, future orientation, and past orientation are physically identical processes.

They are treated as coordinates for comparing different forms of transformation.

The working hypothesis can be expressed as follows:

> Changes of spatial scale may be related to changes of temporal scale.

A second proposition follows:

> Past macroscopic structures may form future environmental conditions through decomposition, dispersion, and miniaturization.

A third proposition concerns the microscopic direction:

> Processes that transform too rapidly or at scales too small for ordinary human perception may become observable only after slowing and re-entry into the middle range.

This provisional relationship is called:

> **Scale–Time Reciprocity**

It is a conceptual model, not an established law of physics.

## 6. Integrating REC and PQIR

The **REC model** considers whether multiple universes, possible world-states, or spacetime conditions can be differentiated by entropy.

“Multiverse” is used here in two related senses:

1. as a speculative reference to multiple universes;
2. as a conceptual framework for comparing multiple world-states with different entropy, time, and scale conditions.

**PQIR** considers whether information related to consciousness or personality can be modeled as moving not only along the ordinary past-to-future causal direction, but also through alternative temporal relations.

When the two models are combined, the following propositions emerge:

1. Matter and information may exhibit different temporal characteristics at different scales.
2. Macroscopic structures may become future microscopic conditions through decomposition and dispersion.
3. Microscopic and rapid processes may become present observations through transformation and slowing.
4. Consciousness may function as a middle-range process that observes, remembers, and interprets these transformations.
5. Creative agency may emerge from interactions among different spatial, temporal, material, and informational scales.

## 8. Hypothesis 2: Retrocausal Transmission of Consciousness-Related Information

This hypothesis asks whether information associated with consciousness can be modeled in a direction other than ordinary forward causality.

The weakest version is cognitive rather than physical. Predictions, expectations, future goals, and anticipated outcomes can influence present decisions and reinterpretations of the past.

A stronger version would examine whether consciousness-related information could be represented as propagating retrocausally along a temporal axis. That stronger claim is not currently established.

## 10. Future Formation through Material Circulation

### 10.1 The Example of Decayed Wood

When decayed wood is burned, the wood loses its macroscopic structure.

The matter does not disappear. Combustion transforms it into products such as:

- carbon dioxide;
- water vapor;
- ash;
- particles;
- heat;
- mineral residues.

These products disperse into the atmosphere, soil, water, and biosphere. Some may later be absorbed by plants, transferred to other organisms, or incorporated into atmospheric and geological cycles.

The empirically ordinary process is the transformation and redistribution of matter.

The conceptual interpretation proposed here is:

> A past macroscopic structure may become part of the future by being dispersed and miniaturized into later environmental conditions.

The phrase “creation of future spacetime” is therefore not presented as established physics. It names a speculative interpretation of how future material conditions arise from past transformation.

---

### 10.2 Past Breath and the Present Atmosphere

Individual molecules exhaled by historical persons cannot ordinarily be identified within the present atmosphere.

However, the atmosphere contains an immense number of molecules and undergoes large-scale mixing over time. It is therefore statistically plausible that some molecules once exhaled by people in the past are present in air inhaled today.

For example, the statement that a present-day person may inhale molecules once exhaled by Albert Einstein is not evidence based on tracing specific molecules. It is a probabilistic inference based on molecular abundance and atmospheric mixing.

The example illustrates how past biological activity may disperse into microscopic components and later participate in future bodies and environments.

---

### 10.3 Earth’s Water and Extraterrestrial Material

The origin of Earth’s water remains an active field of research.

Possible sources include:

- water-bearing asteroids;
- meteorites;
- material retained within the early Earth;
- comets.

It would therefore be too strong to claim that most of Earth’s water was delivered by comets alone.

A more careful statement is:

> Some of Earth’s water may have been supplied by water-bearing asteroids, meteorites, comets, and other early planetary materials.

This example also demonstrates how microscopic material, accumulated over long periods, may contribute to oceans, atmospheres, ecosystems, and life.

## 11. Emergence

In this paper, **emergence** means the appearance of novel macroscopic properties from local interactions.

A single water molecule does not contain a wave. Waves, vortices, and flows arise through interactions among many molecules.

Likewise, a single cell, AI system, person, memory, or record may not contain the global pattern that later emerges through interaction.

This paper therefore considers whether creative agency can emerge from relations among:

- time;
- consciousness;
- matter;
- information;
- observation;
- memory.

## 12. Reframing the Earth and Mars Symbols

The Earth and Mars symbols are not the primary theoretical structure of this paper.

They are secondary visual markers used to make the human middle-range model easier to perceive.

### Earth Symbol: ⊕

The Earth symbol marks the return path in the quantum-facing direction.

It represents microscopic and accelerated transformation becoming observable again after slowing into the human present.

### Mars Symbol: ♂

The Mars symbol marks the outward path in the classical-facing direction.

It represents macroscopic structures moving through accumulation, decay, decomposition, and miniaturization before becoming part of future material conditions.

The symbols do not claim literal planetary causation. They function as visual coordinates for two different scale–time transformation paths.

## 13. Discussion

The central contribution of this concept paper is not the claim that humans occupy the physical center of the universe.

Rather, humans are positioned as a **middle range of observability** between processes that are smaller and faster, and processes that are larger and slower.

This framing suggests that time might be explored not only as a single line, but also as a pattern of transformation, return, dispersion, and re-entry across scales.

The following relations remain speculative and are not established physical laws:

- miniaturization and future orientation;
- expansion and past orientation;
- acceleration and temporal displacement;
- slowing and reappearance in the present;
- material decomposition and spacetime creation;
- retrocausal transmission of consciousness-related information.

Empirically observed processes, such as combustion, atmospheric mixing, and material circulation, must remain clearly distinguished from the philosophical interpretation placed upon them.

This distinction is not a retreat from the hypothesis. It is the boundary that makes the hypothesis examinable rather than decorative.

## 15. Conclusion

This paper places the ordinary human observational world at a middle-range reference point.

From there, it compares:

- a quantum-facing direction of miniaturization, acceleration, and microscopic transformation;
- a classical-facing direction of expansion, slowing, and macroscopic transformation.

In the first direction, microscopic and accelerated processes are hypothesized to become observable after transformation and slowing into the human present.

In the second direction, macroscopic structures are hypothesized to move through accumulation, decay, dispersion, and miniaturization before contributing to future environmental conditions.

These two paths are not presented as an established physical symmetry. They function as conceptual mirrors for exploring relations among time, matter, consciousness, and emergence.

The paper therefore treats time not only as a line, but as something that may be transformed, returned, dispersed, and recomposed across scales.
